# HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

Interactive Power BI dashboard analysing employee attrition for 1,470 employees.

## Key insights
- Overall attrition rate is 16.1% (237 of 1,470 employees).
- Attrition is highest in the 26-35 age group.
- Employees earning under 5K show the highest attrition (163 cases).
- Laboratory Technicians have the most attrition among job roles (62).
- Attrition peaks in the first 1-2 years at the company.

## Features
- KPI cards: employees, attrition, attrition rate, average age, average salary, years at company
- Department filter buttons
- Breakdowns by gender, education field, age, salary slab, tenure and job role

## Tools
Power BI, DAX, data modelling, data visualisation

## Data
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset (public)
